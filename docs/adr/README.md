Numbering gaps are deliberate: 0001/0006 were reserved while wayfinder map #7 was charting and dropped when G1 resolved; keeping their slots means every cross-reference in the map and its tickets stays valid.

**Withdrawn records are kept, not deleted.** A withdrawn ADR is history: it records what was decided, on what evidence, and why it stopped being true — deleting it would leave every cross-reference to it dangling and destroy the reasoning that produced the replacement. Two are withdrawn so far, both by ADR-0034 on 2026-10-10: ADR-0025 (the surface is a setting — the key and the `off` value are gone) and ADR-0030 (the surface is switched by writing the file and reloading — there is no longer a surface to switch). Each names its replacement in its own `status:` header.

**Amended records carry their amendment in the record, not here.** ADR-0016 §2 and ADR-0022 §9 were amended on 2026-09-30 (review round 4): the dispatch concurrency cap is one counter per pi session serving both the foreground and the background front, rather than a per-run cap. ADR-0029 was amended on 2026-10-08 by ADR-0033: its under-reporting bound held, but the premise did not — pi's MCP extension activates `codemode` through a `setActiveTools` call that no settings file records. The titles above are the original decisions; open those records for the amendment, the measurement, and what it costs.

ADR-0037 was amended on 2026-10-10 by the review round over the website work: the record had not stated that projecting documents means the site has **no editable source of its own**, and the consequence shipped as an `editLink` that was dead on every projected page. The amendment sits in the record, under "One correction made during the work" — including the alternative that was measured and rejected (writing each projected file at its repository path and routing by frontmatter `slug`), because VitePress 1.6.4 ignores that `slug`.

**The website added no glossary terms, and that is a stated outcome rather than an oversight.** ADR-0036 through ADR-0038 were written after the site shipped, and `CONTEXT.md` was not touched by any of its commits — `git diff --name-only e768bde~1 1113680 -- CONTEXT.md` is empty. The site introduces no domain concept: it renders existing documentation, and its own vocabulary (landing page, projection, generated page) is build machinery rather than something a reader of this package needs to hold. The one phrase worth checking is the landing page's "generated from" marker, and it names a file path, not a term. If a future change gives the site a concept that appears in the package's _behaviour_ — not just its build — it belongs in `CONTEXT.md` and this paragraph is the place to correct.
