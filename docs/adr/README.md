Numbering gaps are deliberate: 0001/0006 were reserved while wayfinder map #7 was charting and dropped when G1 resolved; keeping their slots means every cross-reference in the map and its tickets stays valid.

**Withdrawn records are kept, not deleted.** A withdrawn ADR is history: it records what was decided, on what evidence, and why it stopped being true — deleting it would leave every cross-reference to it dangling and destroy the reasoning that produced the replacement. Three are withdrawn so far: ADR-0025 (the surface is a setting — the key and the `off` value are gone) and ADR-0030 (the surface is switched by writing the file and reloading — there is no longer a surface to switch), both by ADR-0034 on 2026-10-10; and ADR-0047 (the search is program-internal, in two faces) by ADR-0049 on 2026-10-20 — the same day it was accepted and amended, because the shape it decided did not survive contact with the requirement that the capability belong to the sub-agent. Each names its replacement in its own `status:` header.

**Amended records carry their amendment in the record, not here.** ADR-0016 §2 and ADR-0022 §9 were amended on 2026-09-30 (review round 4): the dispatch concurrency cap is one counter per pi session serving both the foreground and the background front, rather than a per-run cap. ADR-0016 §Decision 1 and the matching field list in ADR-0025 were amended on 2026-10-12 by ADR-0039: `agent` is now optional, and omitting it dispatches a generalist child rather than refusing. ADR-0029 was amended on 2026-10-08 by ADR-0033: its under-reporting bound held, but the premise did not — pi's MCP extension activates `codemode` through a `setActiveTools` call that no settings file records. ADR-0007 was amended on 2026-10-13 by an operator decision: the host's own programmable tool calling supplies the JS-realm boundary, so no sandbox is built here, and the staged plan that would have built one (#71, #72, #74–#81) is closed as superseded rather than re-pointed a third time. ADR-0042 was amended on 2026-10-19 by ADR-0043: its `goal` argument is now `why`, because the agent ecosystem spends `goal` on an objective or a loop, and it gained a default-on `whyEnabled` switch — the evidence table is unchanged and is not re-run, which the amendment states rather than leaving open. ADR-0047 was amended on 2026-10-20, the same day it was accepted, after a live probe on a real pi 1.3.0: the codemode bridge is measured rather than inferred (the static conclusions were not refuted), and "never model-facing" became "**not** model-facing by default" — `tool_search` can find a `codemode`-exposure tool and activate it into the model's declared set, and pi offers no exposure that is program-reachable but not tool-searchable. The decision did not change; the guarantee it promised did. **That record was then withdrawn the same day** by ADR-0049, which replaces the two-face shape with one: the search lives in the dispatched child and the child's own model calls it. Its _premises_ — the retrieval source is fixed, the name carries `ds`, the error text is one shared constant, results pass through unchanged — all survive; the shape and the narrowness promise did not. ADR-0016 §Decision 1/3 and ADR-0022 §3 were amended on 2026-10-19 by ADR-0044: a dispatch that names no model at the call site or in the agent definition inherits the caller session's current model, the model the child actually ran is reported on the result and the record, and the foreground rejection semantics now match the background one. ADR-0025, ADR-0026, ADR-0027, ADR-0028, ADR-0029, ADR-0030, ADR-0032, ADR-0033, ADR-0034, ADR-0035, ADR-0040, ADR-0041, ADR-0047, ADR-0049 and ADR-0050 were amended on 2026-10-21 by ADR-0051: the two surface values are renamed — `full` → `full_ptc`, `subagents` → `codemode_ptc` — and the surface now means _which orchestrator is responsible to the model_. Every decision those records made stands; only the names changed, and no alias was left behind. The titles above are the original decisions; open those records for the amendment, the measurement, and what it costs. ADR-0013, ADR-0016, ADR-0019, ADR-0021, ADR-0022, ADR-0023, ADR-0024, ADR-0025, ADR-0026, ADR-0027, ADR-0028, ADR-0029, ADR-0032, ADR-0033, ADR-0039, ADR-0040, ADR-0041, ADR-0042, ADR-0043, ADR-0044, ADR-0045, ADR-0047, ADR-0049, ADR-0050 and ADR-0053 were amended on 2026-10-21 by ADR-0052: the two task doors are renamed — `pi.dispatch` → `pi_task_ptc` (the PTC door, a binding inside a program) and `ptc_subagent` → `pi_task_codemode` (the codemode door, reachable from a `codemode` script) — with no alias and no compatibility shim, and the word the model reads becomes "task". Every decision those records made stands; only the names changed.

ADR-0037 was amended on 2026-10-10 by the review round over the website work: the record had not stated that projecting documents means the site has **no editable source of its own**, and the consequence shipped as an `editLink` that was dead on every projected page. The amendment sits in the record, under "One correction made during the work" — including the alternative that was measured and rejected (writing each projected file at its repository path and routing by frontmatter `slug`), because VitePress 1.6.4 ignores that `slug`.

**The website added no glossary terms, and that is a stated outcome rather than an oversight.** ADR-0036 through ADR-0038 were written after the site shipped, and the glossary was not touched by any of its commits — `git diff --name-only e768bde~1 1113680 -- CONTEXT.md` is empty (the file was named `CONTEXT.md` at those commits; it is `GLOSSARY.md` today, per ADR-0046). The site introduces no domain concept: it renders existing documentation, and its own vocabulary (landing page, projection, generated page) is build machinery rather than something a reader of this package needs to hold. The one phrase worth checking is the landing page's "generated from" marker, and it names a file path, not a term. If a future change gives the site a concept that appears in the package's _behaviour_ — not just its build — it belongs in `GLOSSARY.md` and this paragraph is the place to correct.
