## What to build

The decision gets written down before any code moves, so that the rest of the work has something to be correct against.

A new ADR records that a dispatched child produces a **child report**: a structured value crossing the child→parent boundary, delivered over one of two channels, with an explicit statement of which channel delivered it. The domain glossary gains a `child report` entry, because the term `structured result` is already taken and means something else here.

The glossary entry must state plainly that `child report` is **not** the existing `structured result` term: that one is `structuredContent`, is defined as never reaching the model, and means the opposite thing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An ADR records the decision, its two channels, and why the tool channel cannot be the only one
- [ ] The ADR is listed in the ADR index alongside its neighbours
- [ ] The glossary gains a `child report` entry naming its shape and its avoidance terms
- [ ] The glossary entry states the relationship to `structured result` explicitly, in both directions
- [ ] The glossary's existing entries are unchanged
